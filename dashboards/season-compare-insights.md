### Spring → Summer: what changed

**1. The division scored a lot more in Summer.**
League run rate 5.76 → 7.35 (+28%); average score 104 → 133.
That is why this page compares ranks, not raw numbers.

**2. Our batting stood still while the league moved.**
Run rate 7.20 → 7.12 and average score 133 → 133.
That made us the best batting side in Spring (1st of 16) and 11th in Summer.

**3. The middle overs are the biggest change.**
Wickets lost in overs 7-15: 2.25 → 4.22 (1st → 11th).
Death-overs scoring fell too: 9.53 → 7.94 (1st → 11th).

**4. The bowling improved, relative to the division.**
Economy rank 15th → 8th, dot balls 16th → 5th, wickets per match 12th → 4th.

**5. Both knockout exits were chases that ended all out.**
Spring QF: 102 all out chasing 120. Summer QF: 133 all out chasing 161.
We were bowled out once in Spring and 4 times in Summer.

**6. More rotation.** Batters used: 22 → 26.

**7. Same result both times:** quarter-finalists (league 5-2 → 5-3).

<small>11 of the 16 Div A teams played both seasons. Spring excludes 5 matches with no result. Source: CricClubs.</small>
